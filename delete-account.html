Delete Your Account

If you wish to delete your Delivery Partner account and all associated data, please send an email to:

Email: supportnanishealthhub@gmail.com

How to Request

  1. Send an email with the subject Delete Account Request.
  2. Include your registered phone number or email address.
  3. Our support team will verify your request.
  4. Your account will be deleted within 7 business days.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Profile Information
  • Personal Details
  • Delivery Partner Account
  • Saved Addresses
  • Chat Messages
  • App Preferences

Data That May Be Retained

Certain records such as payment history, transaction details, tax records, or legal compliance records may be retained for the period required by applicable law.
